Scrollhole — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Scrollhole is a screen-time awareness app. Our privacy policy is short because our data practices are short: everything Scrollhole does happens on your device. Nothing leaves your phone.

What Scrollhole accesses, and why

  • App usage data (Usage Access permission). Scrollhole reads which app is in the foreground and for how long, so it can track your daily time against the budgets you set for the apps you choose to watch. This data is read from Android's built-in usage system, processed on your device, and stored only in Scrollhole's local database.
  • Display over other apps. Scrollhole draws its black-hole overlay above the apps you've chosen to watch once you're past your budget. This permission draws pixels; it cannot read the screen.
  • Notifications. Used for the tracking-status notification, optional weekly recap, and alerts if tracking stops.
  • Installed app list. Scrollhole lists your launchable apps once, on your device, so you can pick which ones to watch. This list is never transmitted anywhere.

What we collect on our servers

Nothing. Scrollhole has no servers, no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We never see your usage data, your app list, your budgets, or anything else.

Data storage and deletion

All Scrollhole data (watched apps, budgets, usage history, settings) lives in app-private storage on your device. Uninstalling Scrollhole deletes all of it. The optional Backup feature writes a file only to a location you pick, on your device, and only when you tap Backup.

Data sharing

We share no data with anyone, because we hold no data. The optional "Share this week" feature composes a text summary that you can choose to send through an app of your choice — that action is entirely under your control.

Children

Scrollhole is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If our data practices ever change, this policy will be updated here and the change will be called out in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
